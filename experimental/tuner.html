💡 How the Kemper Tuner Works over MIDI

  • Tuner Mode Activation: The Kemper Profiler only outputs pitch/tuner data when Tuner Mode is active. Click the Engage Tuner (CC #31) button above, or step on your Kemper's tuner switch.
  • Browser Requirements: Web MIDI requires a secure origin (http://localhost or https://) and works natively in Chrome, Edge, and Opera.
  • UI to MIDI Setting: On the Kemper hardware, open the System Menu and ensure UI to MIDI is enabled so the Profiler broadcasts UI state changes over USB/MIDI.
  • SysEx & Port Confirmation: Watch the Live MIDI Packet Monitor above. When you play a note, any incoming packets (SysEx, CC, or Pitch Bend) will appear immediately in the log.
